The Club

Everything a country club is, indoors

A clubhouse ten minutes from home. A calendar worth planning around. Golf whenever you feel like it, on any course in the world — and a game that keeps getting better because you finally play enough for it to matter.

What we left out: the initiation fee, the tee sheet, the dress code, and the slow play.

This is not a place you use for a few months and let go. It is the club you belong to — open every day of the year, the same key, the same room, the same people.

Members only. Forty of them.

The Clubhouse

One Pin, All Access

Three thousand square feet that belong to forty people and nobody else.

The Simulator Bay

A Uneekor EyeXO 2 and GSPro: Pebble Beach at midnight, St Andrews before work. Real ball data on every swing, whether you came to compete or just to swing freely.

The Members Lounge

Premium seating, big screens, and the bar that turns a round into an evening. Watch a major, hold court on league night, or have the room to yourself.

The Putting Green

Indoor practice year-round. Dial in distance control any night of the year, and settle the short-game series while you are at it.

Meeting Space

A quiet, handsome place to host clients or take a call. Business gets done here, and the club is yours while you do it.

Your Game

The longer you belong, the better you get

Nobody joins a club to be coached at. But every ball you hit here is measured, and a game built on hundreds of rounds a year does not go backward. Members who have been here a season play differently than when they walked in — and the ones who stay keep going.

The Club Year

Twelve Months of Reasons to Be Here

The calendar never goes dark. One league, one social night, and one reason to bring a guest every quarter — published a year ahead.

Sep — Nov

The Northern Kentucky Cup, played as match play. President's Cup and NFL watch party in the lounge.

Dec — Feb

The Club Championship:
ten weeks, three flights, live event finale. Fitting night in February.

Mar — May

Masters week — the club's flagship night. Spring scramble before the outdoor season opens.

Jun — Aug

Fort Tommy Guns Summer League. Rain-night golf, twilight rounds after the course closes, and air conditioning in August.
