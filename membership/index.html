Get Started

How to Join

Four steps, and the longest one takes twenty minutes.

01

Visit the Club

A private walkthrough of the clubhouse. See the space, ask everything, hit a few balls.

02

Choose Your Membership

Founding Member or the Raccoon Rate. Sign on the spot if a place is open.

03

Orientation and PIN

A walkthrough of the simulator and the house rules. Your personal code is issued.

04

Your First Round

Pick a course, get your numbers on the board, and put your name in the next league. Then do it again tomorrow.
